Privacy Policy – Tubora

Developer: Nilevo Studios
Contact: abdelatielshafi@gmail.com
Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Overview

Tubora is an offline puzzle game. We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email, phone number or location. Your game progress is saved only on your device.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions. This is handled by Google under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads from your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads).

3. Sharing

When you choose to share a result or a challenge code, the game opens your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. Data deletion

All game data lives on your device. Uninstalling the game, or using "Reset progress" in settings, removes it.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
