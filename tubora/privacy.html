Developer: Nilevo Studios

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

Tubora is a puzzle game you can play without an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The game does use two Google services, advertising and analytics, which process some device and usage data as described below.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not combine this data with any other source to identify you.

3. Your consent and choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form on first launch. Analytics stays switched off until you have answered that form, and it stays off if you do not allow storing or accessing information on your device. Where the form is required you can reopen it at any time with the Ad Choices button on the main menu.

On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in the device settings (Settings › Google › Ads).

4. Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a few reminders on your own device (for example that the daily gift is ready). They are created locally: no data is sent to us or to anyone else to deliver them. You can turn them off at any time in your device's notification settings for Tubora.

5. Game progress

Your progress, coins and settings are saved on your device. If device backup is switched on in your Android settings, Android may include this save in the backup of your Google account so that it can be restored on a new device. We have no access to that backup.

6. Sharing

When you choose to share a result or a challenge code, the game opens your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Keeping and deleting data

Uninstalling the game removes everything it saved on your device. Advertising and analytics data is kept by Google for the periods set in its services; it is linked to your advertising ID or to an app-instance identifier, not to your name. Resetting your advertising ID and reinstalling the game breaks that link. If you want us to request deletion of the analytics data associated with your installation, write to the contact address above and we will help.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and changes are posted on this page.
