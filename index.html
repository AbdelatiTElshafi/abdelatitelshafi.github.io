Tubora Game

A ball-sort puzzle on a trip down the Nile.

Sort the colored balls until every tube holds a single color. Travel from Aswan to Alexandria, collect golden artifacts, and keep Mishmish the cat company along the way.

  • Eight regions across Egypt and Greece, and a fresh daily puzzle every day.
  • Challenge a friend with a short code. No account needed.
  • Play in English or Arabic.
  • Free to play, with short ads between levels and optional videos when you want a hint.

How we build

Calm first

No countdown timers and no pressure. A level should feel like a deep breath, not a chase.

Egypt, told gently

We draw from the places and objects we grew up around, and share short facts about them that you can skip.

Fair to players

Games are free to start. Ads stay short and spaced out, and rewards come from playing.
